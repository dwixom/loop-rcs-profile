Messaging terms

Effective November 13, 2026.

Program and participation

Loop is a closed RCS communications pilot for founder-directed company work: assignments, questions, decisions, results, and status updates. Only invited devices that accept the sender's test invitation may participate. This page is not public enrollment. By separately opting into the pilot and accepting its invitation, you request these messages to the device you control and authorize the processing described in the privacy notice, including the messaging platform processing needed to provide the service.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with your conversation, pending questions, and material work updates. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. This pilot offers no purchase on this site and uses RCS with SMS fallback disabled.

Stop, start, and help

Reply STOP to stop messages, START to opt back in, and HELP for assistance. For additional operator support, contact the operator through the private channel used for your invitation and identify the request as a Loop pilot support request. An opt-in does not automatically replay old requests or expired choices. Use a separate pause or cancel task instruction to change actual work.

Results and controls

Demonstration outputs are labeled Demo and are used to test communications behavior. They do not establish a real specialist assessment. A reply control applies only to its bound task and current proposal. Selecting a direction does not itself grant spending, publication, or deployment authority; actual operations remain subject to Loop's current execution policy. Delivery and read receipts are not approvals.

Network delivery

Delivery depends on network availability. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Pilot limits

The pilot may be interrupted or changed during testing. Do not rely on it for emergency communication or send credentials, payment data, or sensitive identifiers. Review consequential results and use the existing operator channel if the pilot does not respond. Google and other messaging services remain governed by their own applicable terms; these pilot terms do not replace them.
