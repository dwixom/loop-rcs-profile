Invitation-only communications pilot

Privacy notice

Effective November 13, 2026. This notice covers the closed Loop RCS pilot described on this site.

Information used

The pilot processes the invited recipient's phone number, messages and supplied attachment references, selected reply controls, company and work context, and messaging records such as sender, conversation, delivery, and event identifiers. The gateway keeps a private transcript and communication state so it can route replies, recover interrupted work, and prevent duplicate effects.

Purpose and access

Information is used to deliver the requested conversations, coordinate scoped work, maintain pending questions, investigate failures, and test the pilot. Company role assignments receive permitted task context and opaque references. The gateway's contact mapping, provider credentials, and cross-company transcript are kept outside those bounded assignments. The operator can inspect pilot records for troubleshooting.

Messaging privacy and frequency

We do not sell or rent personal information. No mobile information, phone numbers, or messaging opt-in data and consent are shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information needed to provide messaging is processed by the service providers described below. Message frequency varies with the conversation and work updates. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop messages or HELP for assistance.

Messaging services

RCS delivery uses Cadence, Google, and the mobile messaging network. Those services process the information needed to carry messages and operate the channel under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Google may review messaging activity and process message content for spam detection and prevention, including its associated models, as described in Google's RCS for Business terms. Invited participants should understand this processing before joining.

Retention and requests

Conversation records, event identities, decisions, and work evidence are retained for pilot operation and recovery under the operator's existing retention and backup practices. No fixed deletion period is promised by this pilot notice. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the operator through the private channel used for your invitation and identify the request as a Loop pilot privacy request. The operator will review the request, including any records that must remain for security, audit, or work continuity.

Your choices

Participation is optional. Reply STOP to stop further messages; START may restore contactability. Stopping messages does not automatically cancel underlying work or erase saved records. Do not send passwords, API keys, payment credentials, or sensitive identifiers in the conversation. You may ask the operator to stop a task separately.

This website

This static pilot information site has no enrollment form, advertising, or application analytics code. Its hosting service may process ordinary network request information. Updated pilot information will be posted here; the main website is being built separately.
